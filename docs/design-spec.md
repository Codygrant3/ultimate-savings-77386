# Savings Desk MVP design specification

## Product promise

Savings Desk is a calm, evidence-first command center for ZIP code 77386. It ranks opportunities by meaningful value, not promotion volume.

## Brand

- Name: Savings Desk
- Working role: Personal Chief Savings Officer
- Voice: Practical, selective, transparent
- Primary: forest `#0f4c3a`
- Accent: warm orange `#d9683c`
- Highlight: gold `#f7c95f`
- Canvas: warm cream `#f4f2ec`
- Headings: Manrope
- Body: DM Sans

## Information architecture

1. Overview: current potential, expiring offers, saved offers, and realized savings.
2. All opportunities: ranked, searchable, and filterable deal inventory.
3. Rewards programs: official loyalty programs and clearly marked research leads.
4. Monday briefing: a short action plan for the week.

## Evidence contract

Every opportunity must include:

- An official or clearly described source.
- The date the source was checked.
- One of three states: `verified`, `program`, or `needs-check`.
- Any required spend.
- An expiration date when known.
- A local or eligibility caution when offers can vary.

The product must not convert an old chat claim into a current offer without verification.

## Ranking contract

The score combines:

- Official evidence quality.
- Estimated dollar value.
- Percentage savings.
- Time sensitivity.
- Redemption effort.
- Required-spend penalty.

Expired deals are removed before ranking. Programs and research leads remain visible, but they cannot outrank strong verified savings merely because they might produce a future freebie.

## Interaction contract

- Save an opportunity locally.
- Mark an opportunity as used and add its estimated value to realized savings.
- Search by merchant, offer, or tag.
- Filter by savings category.
- Open the original source in a new tab.
- Preserve usability at 375 px and up.

## Visual direction

The interface uses editorial typography, compact financial cards, restrained shadows, and a dark forest workspace rail. It avoids coupon-site clutter, loud gradients, and promotional imagery.
